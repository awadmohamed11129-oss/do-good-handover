What you are getting, and how it has been running

Three pieces. The numbers are from real runs; every person in them is made up.

How it fits together

The contractor form

Runs by itself

People upload a résumé and answer a few questions. Gemini reads the résumé, a row lands in your Sheet, and a folder always holds the current list with the résumé PDFs.

How it has been running

  • 20of 20 test sign-ups were read by Gemini in 3 to 5 seconds each (18 November, one a minute).
  • 28made-up people sit in the Sheet. 0 are waiting to be read. 5 unnamed test rows are still marked Started (nobody finished them).
  • ~1 minafter someone finishes, the folder for the app is updated. It can trail the newest one or two people by a few minutes.
See a real example
  • Made-upSophie Tremblay uploads a PDFShe answers about eight quick questions. The form builds her profile: content strategist and UX writer, Montréal, 8.2 years, French and English, $70 an hour. Her row appears in the Sheet and her résumé is copied into the folder as TAL-INT-ON-0xx.pdf.

Strengths

  • A contractor never sees an error. If Gemini is busy they type a name and an email, and the résumé is read later.
  • The same email updates the same row, so there are no duplicates.
  • The originals are never touched. The app gets copies.

Honest limits

  • People signed in to two Google accounts can get “Page Not Found” on the plain link. A Google Site page fixes it on desktop browsers; phones are not confirmed yet.
  • Anyone who knows a contractor’s email can overwrite that profile. The older version is kept.
  • It holds made-up people only. No real contractor has used it yet.

Strengths

  • Rules, not AI, and no key. It cannot run up a bill.
  • You can see why. Every job has a “why kept” line, and the “Dropped today” tab lists what was skipped and why.
  • The lists are cells in the Sheet: sources, blocked words, role words, settings. You can edit them.

Honest limits

  • Rules can misjudge a title. Some good jobs rank low or get skipped: a 6-month Kabam contract was dropped because it was 92 days old and the limit is 90.
  • It learns which roles to favour from your contractor list. Today that list is about 28 made-up people, so recruiter roles are over-represented until real contractors join.
  • Public sources only. No LinkedIn or Indeed, which block this kind of reading.

What is switched off on purpose

Each of these is off today. All sit in one place: the Setup tab of the contractor Sheet lists them. The three e-mails (new profile, daily summary, something broke) you turn on with one menu item, Contractor intake, then Email alerts (on / off), if your menu shows it; if not, set the script properties named below. The others are advanced switches in Extensions, Apps Script, Project Settings, Script properties.

Alert emails
NOTIFY_EMAIL_ON
Turn on with Contractor intake, then Email alerts (on / off), if your menu shows it: you type your address and answer yes or no. Otherwise set NOTIFY_EMAIL to your address and NOTIFY_EMAIL_ON to true. An email each time a profile is finished. Test sign-ups (an address with +test or @example.com) never reach you.
Daily summary
DIGEST_EMAIL_ON
One short email a day, only on days with news. Same menu item (Email alerts (on / off)) or set the property to true.
Update-my-profile link
EDIT_LINK_ON
Adds a personal link, valid 60 days, to the thank-you email so a contractor can edit their profile. A security review said to keep it off.
Two extra columns
EXPORT_EXTRA_COLS
Adds availability and profile_updated to the contractor file. Wilson’s released app refuses unknown columns; the improved app accepts them. Turn on only after he confirms.
Profession list follows the app
PROF_SYNC_ON
Once a day the profession list copies the newest one from the app. It changes what Gemini picks from, so ask Wilson first.
Collector: two extra CSV columns
EXPORT_CSV_EXTRA
On the Settings tab. Adds why_kept and fits_roles to the daily CSV. The released app refuses a CSV with unknown columns.
Collector: day folders
DAY_FOLDERS
On the Settings tab. yes puts each day’s files in a dated folder.

The collector never sends email. Only the contractor form can.

If something looks wrong
The first line of a Start here tab says “Action needed”
Open the Setup tab and do what the red line says. For the collector you can also click Job collector, then Run now.
Yellow: “Some sources failed”
Do nothing. They are tried again at the next run. If the same source fails three days in a row, open the Sources tab and type no in its on cell.
A contractor says “Page Not Found” or “unable to open the file”
They are signed in to two Google accounts. Ask them to open the link in a private window, or send them the Google Site link.
Contractor Sheet is red: no Gemini key, or the key is not accepted
Menu Contractor intake, then 2. Add the Gemini key, and paste a working one. Nothing is lost while it waits.
The app says “not found” when you press Get latest
The download link was mistyped or replaced. Copy it again from the Setup tab of the contractor Sheet. If it was shared by mistake, use Contractor intake, Make a new app link, and paste the new one.
Too few jobs, or too many weak ones
On the collector’s Settings tab change MIN_FIT_SCORE (45 now): lower gives more jobs, higher gives fewer. MAX_NEW_PER_DAY (20) caps the day.

Going back to the previous version

  • A setting or a list in a Sheet: File, Version history, See version history, pick an earlier day, Restore this version. This restores cells, not code.
  • The form’s code: Extensions, Apps Script, Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, choose an earlier version, Deploy. The form link does not change.
  • Before any change you are unsure about: make a copy of the Sheet first (File, Make a copy). The copy is your safe version.
Known limits in full

Contractor form

  • “Page Not Found” for people signed in to two Google accounts. A Google Site page that holds the form fixes it on desktop browsers (tested in Edge with several accounts signed in, signed out, and on a phone-sized screen). Real iPhone Safari and Firefox with two accounts are not tested. On a phone the form scrolls inside its box.
  • The personal “update my profile” links still use the plain Google link.
  • Anyone who knows a contractor’s email can overwrite that profile by sending the form again. The older version is kept in a hidden tab.
  • The résumé folder can trail the newest one or two people by a few minutes. Nothing is lost.
  • Word files become a plain-text PDF (the layout is lost). Photos from iPhones in HEIC or WebP format cannot be turned into PDF, so those people have no résumé in the app folder. Their original is kept.
  • A free Gemini key: Google may use what you send it to improve its products and asks people not to send personal information. For real résumés, a key with billing on is the safer choice.

Job collector

  • Rules, not AI. A good job with an odd title can be missed, and some good jobs rank low or are skipped.
  • It favours roles your contractor list holds. Today that is about 28 made-up people.
  • Public sources only: company career pages and open feeds. No LinkedIn, Indeed or Job Bank.
  • One of the feeds (Himalayas) has terms of use that do not clearly cover agency use. Each of its job files carries a credit line and the original link. Switch it off with USE_HIMALAYAS = no.

The app

  • No Stop button for a search. Closing the app in the middle is safe.
  • I never ran a real Gemini search on the improved screens, only on Wilson’s released version.
  • Only tried on Windows. Not tried on a Mac or in Docker. On a Mac the first open needs one extra confirmation (Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway); on Windows unzip it first, then click More info, then Run anyway.

The handover itself

  • I tried “copy both Sheets and click Set up now” in pieces on copies (the collector part, start to finish, on 24 November). The last click, Google’s permission window on a new account, only the account owner can make, so that one step was not seen end to end.
The full setup steps, in order

Nothing here can break the originals: you only ever work in your own copies. The first time you click a menu item, Google shows “Authorization required”. Click OK, pick your account, click Advanced, then “Go to … (unsafe)”, then Allow. It asks once per Sheet. It says unsafe only because you own the script and Google has not reviewed it.

If Google ever says “Sorry, unable to open the file” or “Page Not Found”, you are signed in to two accounts. Open a private window and sign in to only the account that owns the Sheet.

The contractor Sheet

  1. Open the contractor Sheet. File, Make a copy, name it, click Make a copy.
  2. In your copy, menu Contractor intake, then 1. Set up now. Do the permission window, then click the item again. If it asks who is already in the Sheet, click YES (start empty).
  3. Publish the form, the one step Google does not allow a script to do: Extensions, Apps Script, Deploy, New deployment, the gear, Web app. Set Execute as Me and Who has access Anyone. Deploy, and allow if asked.
  4. Back in the Sheet: Contractor intake, then Check again. If the “Form link” line is still red, copy the web app address (it ends in /exec), click 3. Paste the form link and paste it.
  5. On the first tab, Setup, every line should say OK or OPTIONAL. A line that says TO DO tells you what to click.
  6. Click Contractor intake, then 2. Add the Gemini key, paste your key, OK. It is stored privately and never shown in a cell. Click Check again.

The Setup tab now shows two links. The link to send to contractors is the one you give out. The download link goes into the app once. Treat it like a password.

The collector Sheet

  1. Open the collector Sheet. File, Make a copy, name it, click Make a copy.
  2. In your copy, menu Job collector, then 1. Set up now. Do the permission window and click again. When it asks which job list to start with, click YES (a clean list, recommended). It switches on the timers (every morning, with a catch-up later in the morning) and makes the folder “Job collector exports” in your Drive.
  3. Click Job collector, then Run now. The first run takes about three minutes. When it says “The run is finished”, the Setup line “First run done” turns OK.
  4. Click Job collector, then 2. Connect the contractor list. Paste the link of YOUR contractor Sheet (the copy from step 1). Leave it empty and the collector still works with its built-in list of roles.
  5. One click only you can make: open the tab Talent roles (auto), click cell A1, click Allow access. Then Job collector, Check again.

If the Setup line “Export folder ready” says the Drive service is not switched on, do what it says: Extensions, Apps Script, the + next to Services, Drive API, Add. Then click Check again.

Join them and finish

  1. On the collector’s Setup tab, copy the link next to Job files folder. In the contractor Sheet click Contractor intake, then 4. Set the jobs folder, paste it, OK.
  2. On the contractor Sheet’s Setup tab copy the download link. In the app, open Settings, paste it under “Your do good link” and click Test link. It should say how many contractors and jobs it found. (In Wilson’s released version, load the two folders by hand instead.)

You are done when

  • Both Setup tabs show OK or OPTIONAL on every line.
  • The folder “Job collector exports” holds job files after the first run.
  • The app’s test says it found contractors and jobs.

From then on the collector runs by itself every morning. Look at the top line of its Start here tab: “All good” means nothing to do.

Last, send contractors one link

Give contractors the “link to send to contractors” from the Setup tab, and only that one. If you set up the Google Site page, paste its address with Contractor intake, Paste the contractor page link (Google Site), and the Setup tab will show it there.

Settings you may touch

Job collector, tab Settings

MAX_NEW_PER_DAY 20
The most jobs saved as files in a day. The rest wait for the next day.
MIN_FIT_SCORE 45
Jobs scoring under this are not saved. Lower for more, higher for fewer.
MAX_JOBS_PER_COMPANY_PER_DAY 2
Stops one company filling the day. The others wait on the “Waiting for tomorrow” list.

Loading by hand, if the app does not have Get latest

Both folders are plain Google Drive folders and the files are the same either way.

  1. In Drive, right-click Contractors (always current), choose Download, and unzip it. The app refuses a zip, so pick the folder that directly holds contractors.csv.
  2. In the app, Find matches, box 1, Choose folder, pick that folder.
  3. For jobs, open Job collector exports in Drive, select this morning’s .txt files and Download. In the app, box 2, Choose files.
  4. Press Find matches. A job already in the app is skipped, so adding it again is harmless.
